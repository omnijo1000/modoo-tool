The 90-minute rule

Waking up mid-cycle
is why you feel groggy.

Sleep runs in ~90-minute cycles. Wake at the end of one, not the middle.

Waking up at 7:00 AM —
here's when to sleep.

9:45 PM 6 cycles · 9h 15m
11:15 PM 5 cycles · 7h 45m
12:45 AM 4 cycles · 6h 15m
Based on a 15-minute average time to fall asleep
